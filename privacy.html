Privacy Policy

Vision 4K v3.3 and Vision 4K Pro v3.4 (Android) · Effective date: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Vision 4K and Vision 4K Pro apps ("the app", "we", "us") uses and why. We keep it to the minimum needed to run the app and to manage activation.

Vision 4K and Vision 4K Pro are media players only. It does not provide, host or sell any channels, movies, series or other content. You add your own playlist (M3U link or Xtream Codes login) from a provider you are legally allowed to use.

1. Information we collect

When the app starts, it contacts our activation server and sends:

  • A device identifier generated from your Android device ID. The server turns it into a device ID (a MAC-style address and a key) that identifies your installation for activation.
  • App version and the type of device (Android).

Like any internet request, our server also receives your IP address. We store the last IP address and the country derived from it, together with activation dates (trial start and expiry), so that we can manage activation and prevent misuse. To find the country, our server may use a third-party IP lookup service.

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, location (GPS), photos, microphone or camera data.

2. Information stored only on your device

The following stays on your device and is not sent to us:

  • Your playlists, including any server address, username and password you enter
  • Favorites, recently viewed items and playback positions
  • App settings, including your parental control PIN

You can remove this data at any time by deleting playlists inside the app, clearing history in Settings, or uninstalling the app.

3. Your playlist provider

When you play content, the app connects directly to the server of your playlist provider to load channel lists, programme guides and streams. That provider receives your IP address and the login details you entered, under its own privacy policy. We do not control, and are not responsible for, third-party providers or the content they offer.

4. How we use information

  • To activate the app and manage the free trial and subscription period
  • To show messages and update notices that the administrator sends to the app
  • To keep the service secure and to prevent abuse

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising. The app contains no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

5. Sharing

We do not share the information above with third parties, except where required by law or to protect against abuse. The country lookup mentioned in section 1 is the only third-party service used by our server for this purpose.

6. Permissions

  • Internet - to connect to your playlist provider and to our activation server
  • Network state - to show a clear message when there is no connection

7. Data retention and deletion

Device activation records are kept while your activation is active and for a reasonable time afterwards. You can ask us to delete the record linked to your device at any time (see Contact below). Please include the device ID shown in the app under Settings, Device Info.

8. Security

Connections to our activation server use standard web protocols. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Some playlist providers use unencrypted (http) addresses; that is outside our control.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. A parental control option is available in Settings.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version and its effective date will always be available on this page.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy or to request deletion of your activation record, contact us on WhatsApp: +44 7727 188441. You can also reach us from the app: Settings, Contact / Renew.
